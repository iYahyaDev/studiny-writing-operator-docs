# Studiny Writing Operator — Terms of Use

Studiny makes this private Integration available to its authorized operator for internal verification of Studiny Writing deployment metadata. Permission is limited to the approved project, operation, and read scopes. The Studiny owner approves any extension of access separately.

The operator may use the Integration only for this purpose. Access is non-transferable. Sharing, redistributing, or granting a third party access requires prior owner authorization. The operator must safeguard credentials, use the approved protected storage, and report suspected exposure promptly through the Studiny contact channel.

The Integration is available on an as-needed basis. Continuous availability and uninterrupted access are not guaranteed. Studiny may suspend or terminate access at any time. When access ends, the operator must stop using the Integration and cooperate with uninstalling it and retiring its credentials.
